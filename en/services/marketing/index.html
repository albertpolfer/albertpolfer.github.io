The product

ReportAuto Marketing

Who it is for

For marketing agencies and freelancers who stop production at the start of every month to build reports for every client: export, paste into the template, refresh the charts and rewrite the same conclusions with different figures.

What you get back

With 15 accounts and an hour per report, that is 15 hours a month nobody bills. And they are your senior people's hours, which are the expensive ones.

How it works

3 steps

STEP 01

Export and upload

The CSV or Excel you already pull from your analytics tool, your ad platform or your tracking sheet. The system recognises the columns and remembers each account's format.

STEP 02

The system calculates and compares

Change against last month, target attainment, cost per result and performance by channel. It flags whatever falls outside the usual range before generating anything.

STEP 03

Download the report

A PDF carrying your agency's brand, ready to send to the client or to take into the monthly meeting.

Contents

What every report includes

  • Cover page with your agency's brand and the period
  • Executive summary with the key figures of the month
  • Change against last month and the same month last year
  • Performance by channel and by campaign
  • Target attainment and variances
  • Cost per result and cost movement
  • Detected anomalies: drops, spikes and campaigns out of range
  • Analysis and recommendations written in plain language

Supported formats

Excel (.xlsx) and CSV: exports from the usual analytics and advertising platforms, and your own tracking sheet with the columns you already use.

The system does the maths, not the AI.

Variances, attainment and cost per result come from a deterministic engine over your real data. The AI only writes the analysis, over numbers that have already been verified: it will not invent a percentage in front of your client.

Advantages

Why a general-purpose AI is not enough

Because your data arrives ready

The system reads the export as it is and identifies the columns itself. No pasting tables into a chat every month, no re-explaining what each client's metrics mean.

Because the report carries your brand

Every report comes out with your agency's identity and the same structure. The client receives a recognisable document, not something different each month.

Because the percentages are exact

Variances and cost per result are computed by a deterministic engine. A wrong percentage in a client report costs you the account.

Because it scales with your book

Fifteen reports cost the same to prepare as one. Growing your client list stops meaning growing your reporting hours.

Data protection

Your clients' data, protected by design

  • Uploaded files are deleted as soon as the report is generated
  • Only aggregated metrics ever reach the AI
  • No client names, no contact details, no campaign identifiers
  • Each account can only see its own reports
  • Data processing agreement included on signing

Your clients' performance data is commercially sensitive. The system is built so it leaves the server as little as possible: the calculation engine works with it, the language model only sees aggregated figures.

Scope: ReportAuto is a document automation tool. It produces reports from the data you supply and fits into the agency's workflow, alongside its professional judgement.
