The product

ReportAuto Property

Who it is for

For property management firms and block managers who repeat the same work every month: pulling data, calculating arrears, summarising expenses and rewriting the same report with different figures.

What you get back

With 30 buildings and 45 minutes per report, that is more than 20 hours a month spent copying and pasting numbers. ReportAuto turns those hours into billable time.

How it works

3 steps

STEP 01

Upload your files

The Excel or CSV your management software already exports. The system recognises the columns on its own and remembers the format of each building.

STEP 02

The system calculates and verifies

Balances, arrears by age and variances against budget. If something does not add up, it points at the exact row before generating anything.

STEP 03

Download the report

A typeset PDF carrying your brand, ready to send to the board or the owners. The download starts on its own.

Contents

What every report includes

  • Cover page with the building details and the period
  • Statement of accounts: income, expenditure, result and closing balance
  • Arrears per unit, with amount and age of the debt
  • Debt split by age bracket and collection rate
  • Expenditure by category against the approved budget
  • Anomalies and unusual entries detected automatically
  • Analysis and observations written in plain language

Supported formats

Excel (.xlsx) and CSV, in the formats the usual management packages export: European or English number formats, debit and credit columns, and headers under any naming convention.

The system does the maths, not the AI.

Every figure in the report comes from a calculation engine covered by more than a hundred automated tests. The AI only writes the analysis section, and only over numbers that have already been verified.

Advantages

Why a general-purpose AI is not enough

Because your data arrives ready

ReportAuto reads the file your management software exports and identifies the columns itself. No copying data, no re-explaining the context every month, no remembering which format each building's spreadsheet was in.

Because the format never changes

Every report comes out with the same professional structure and your branding, ready to send. Owners receive the same recognisable document month after month.

Because the numbers are exact

Balances, arrears and variances are computed by a deterministic engine over your real data, accurate to the cent. The AI does not touch a single figure.

Because it flags what does not add up

If the declared balance does not match the entries, or a date cannot be read, the system points at the exact row and refuses to generate the report until it is fixed.

Data protection

Your clients' data, protected by design

  • Uploaded files are deleted as soon as the report is generated
  • No personal data ever reaches the AI: only aggregated figures
  • No owner names, no unit identifiers, no building name
  • Each account can only see its own reports
  • Data processing agreement included on signing

An arrears report contains personal data belonging to third parties. The system is built so that data leaves the server as little as possible: the calculation engine works with it, the language model never sees it.

Scope: ReportAuto is a document automation tool. It produces reports from the data you supply and fits into the firm's workflow, alongside its professional judgement.
