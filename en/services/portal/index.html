What you do in the portal

Four steps, once a month

  1. Add the clientOnly the first time. A name and, if you want, your internal reference.
  2. Upload the month's filesExcel or CSV, exactly as your software exports them.
  3. Review the warningsThe portal checks the data adds up and points at anything questionable with the exact row, before generating the report.
  4. Download the PDFThe download starts on its own and the report is kept in your history.

The system does the maths, not the AI.

Figures come from calculation over your numbers. The language model only writes the analysis section, and only receives aggregated data.

Your data

Protected by design

Uploaded files are deleted as soon as the report is generated. No copy is kept.

No personal data ever reaches the AI. It only receives aggregated figures to write the analysis. The system's engine does the calculations.

Each account can only see its own reports, and the data processing agreement is included on signing.
