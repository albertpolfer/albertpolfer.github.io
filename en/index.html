About me

Infrastructure that can be explained, repeated and handed over

I am a systems engineer and I work the full infrastructure cycle: design, deployment and operation on Azure and Microsoft 365, with Windows Server and Linux underneath. Currently at M&G Consulting, in Barcelona.

My rule is always the same: if an environment cannot be rebuilt from scratch, it is not finished. That is why I write infrastructure as code, validate before applying, and leave operations in scripts anyone on the team can read.

I work to a method: a pilot first, then verification against a reference, and only then the rollout across the fleet. That is what makes it possible to touch fifty machines without opening fifty sessions.

I also build ReportAuto, a family of document automation products for sectors that repeat the same report every month. See the product.
